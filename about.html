About Transport 2D Sénégal

A Senegalese company specialising in high-performance petroleum logistics. It works in a liberalised market that the State regulates closely, and holds the official approvals that keep it in line with the sector's strictest standards.

A reference in hydrocarbon transport in Senegal

Transport 2D carries refined petroleum products by road for oil companies, fuel distributors, industrial clients and commercial clients. Product is collected at the depot and delivered to service stations, industrial sites and approved commercial destinations.

Its strength is its people: an experienced management team, senior staff and technicians used to oil-industry standards, and professional drivers trained in the safety of dangerous goods. The team is recognised by the oil majors as one of the best in Senegal.

Our commitments

Four commitments that apply to every load and every contract.

01

Safety

Safety comes first: strict HSE procedures, pre-departure checks, planned routes and continuous driver training. The objective is zero incidents and the protection of people, property and the environment.

02

Reliability

Proactive fleet maintenance and operational discipline, for on-time deliveries without interruption for the whole length of the contract.

03

Innovation

Investment in tracking and optimisation technology to work more efficiently and reduce environmental impact.

04

Partnership

Lasting relationships of trust with the major national and regional energy companies.
