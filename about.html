About Transport 2D Sénégal

Transport 2D Sénégal SASU is a Senegalese road haulage company specialising in refined hydrocarbons and petroleum products. Based at Km 14, Route de Rufisque in Dakar, it supports Senegal's energy supply chain with safe, reliable and efficient fuel transport.

La compagnie qui défie le temps et la distance.

The company that takes on time and distance. The line from the Transport 2D logo.

What the company does

Transport 2D carries refined petroleum products by road for oil companies, fuel distributors, industrial clients and commercial clients. Product is collected at the depot and delivered to service stations, industrial sites and approved commercial destinations.

That work sits in the downstream part of the petroleum chain: the step between the depot and the place where fuel is used or sold.
