    Questions clients ask

    Answers marked To confirm need the company's wording before launch.

    Which petroleum products does Transport 2D transport?

    Diesel and heavy fuel oil, which travel in Transport 2D's own tankers. Other refined products are limited to those the company is authorised and equipped to carry. Petrol and lubricants to confirm

    Which cities and regions are served?

    All of Senegal from Dakar, plus The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea (Conakry) and Mali on the Dakar-Bamako corridor. You can ask about a specific region from the service areas map.

    Is there a minimum transport volume?

    To confirm

    Can I request a one-off delivery?

    Yes. One-off, scheduled and recurring deliveries can all be requested with this form.

    Are recurring delivery contracts available?

    Yes. Recurring logistics agreements and long-term transport contracts are available. Choose "Recurring" and an estimated contract duration in the form.

    How long does it take to receive a quote?

    To confirm

    How are safety and compliance managed?

    Through driver training, vehicle inspections, loading and unloading checks and journey planning. The safety and compliance page covers each point.

    Which documents do I need to provide?

    To confirm

    Can I request a dedicated fleet?

    Yes. Tick "Dedicated fleet service" in section 05 and describe your volumes and sites in the notes.

    Can you handle large or regional volumes?

    Yes. Beyond the 15 own tankers, 50 partner tankers under framework contracts can be mobilised on notice for volume peaks and regional corridors, all coordinated by Transport 2D.

    How are delivery schedules confirmed?

    Before the job, the team confirms the vehicle, driver, route and schedule with you. After delivery you receive a delivery confirmation and the service documents.

    Do you accept partnership requests and tenders?

    Yes. Use the partnerships page to send a proposal or tender details.

    How do I reach the operations team?

    On WhatsApp or by phone at +221 78 130 01 98, or at the office on +221 33 823 63 48, Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. Operational support runs 24/7.
