Safety and compliance

How Transport 2D protects its drivers, clients, other road users, the product and the environment on every delivery.

Draft wording. Transport 2D's HSSE lead should review every line on this page before launch.

Health, safety, security and environment

01People and training
Driver training
Training for tanker drivers on product handling, defensive driving and what to do in an emergency.
Personal protective equipment
Hard hats, high-visibility clothing, gloves and safety footwear at depots and delivery sites.
Site access rules
Drivers follow each depot's and client site's rules on access, parking, speed and permits.
02Vehicles
Vehicle inspections
Checks on tyres, brakes, lights, valves, hoses and safety equipment before a vehicle goes out.
Preventive maintenance
Servicing on a fixed schedule, so parts are replaced before they fail.
03Product handling
Product handling procedures
Written steps for each product the company carries.
Loading and unloading checks
Earthing, valve and hose checks before transfer, with the driver at the vehicle for the whole transfer.
Fire prevention
No ignition sources near transfer points and fire extinguishers within reach.
Spill prevention and management
Equipment and steps to contain, clean up and report any leak.
04Journeys and security
Journey management
Each trip is planned with departure time, stops and expected arrival before the truck leaves.
Route risk assessment
Known hazards on the route are reviewed when the journey is planned.
Road safety
Speed, rest and fatigue rules for drivers on every trip.
Security procedures
In place for vehicles and loads. The details stay internal and are not published here.
05Response, environment and compliance
Emergency response
Steps and contacts for drivers and the office if an incident happens on the road or on site.
Incident reporting
Incidents and near misses are recorded and reviewed so they are not repeated.
Environmental protection
Preventing product loss to soil, water and air during transport and transfer.
Regulatory compliance
Operating under the applicable transport, petroleum product, safety and environmental requirements in Senegal.
